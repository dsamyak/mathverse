The final product should feel like a modern AAA educational experience rather than a traditional learning website.

Every page should create a sense of exploration, curiosity, and immersion. Mathematics should be represented through interactive 3D worlds, dynamic simulations, and cinematic animations that encourage experimentation instead of passive learning.

The interface must be polished enough to serve as a flagship portfolio project, with production-ready architecture, reusable components, responsive design, optimized rendering, and smooth 60 FPS interactions across desktop and mobile devices. The end result should resemble a premium product from companies like Apple, Spline, or Linear while remaining intuitive and genuinely educational.
